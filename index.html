How to read these reports

Budgets show only part of the picture.

Staff can organize, coordinate and deliver programming, even when a budget does not label those costs as “programming.” TownLines reports what public records identify, describes what residents are offered, and says plainly when the available documents do not reveal who paid for an individual activity.
